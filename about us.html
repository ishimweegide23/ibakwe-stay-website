Our Journey

The Ibakwe Stay Story

Ibakwe Stay was born in 2025 from a simple yet powerful vision: to transform how people find and experience homes in Kigali. Founded by Kamugisha Aimable, our journey began with the belief that everyone deserves a place to call home.

The name "Ibakwe" - meaning "to build" in Kinyarwanda - reflects our commitment to building not just houses, but communities, trust, and lasting relationships. What started as a small team with big dreams has grown into Rwanda's most trusted real estate platform.

Today, we serve thousands of clients across Kigali's neighborhoods, connecting landlords with quality tenants, helping families find their dream homes, and providing premium short-stay experiences for visitors to our beautiful country.
